# Reviewer 2

We thank the reviewer for their comments and suggestions, and in particular for pointing out what they describe as the “timely” nature of our dataset:

> The authors missed the dataset FishforKnowledge in their review of previous datasets.

We have included FishforKnowledge in table 1 of our paper; however, we did not include a detailed description of this data set in the “related works” section of the paper due to the fact that the data does not include complex behavioral annotations such as those contained in wildfin, the analysis of this dataset focuses solely on trajectories and the unsupervised clustering of these trajectories. Futhermore, this dataset does not contain expert-generated ground truths for use in the evaluation of methods. We can highlight this fact and the differences among our datasets in the final version of our paper. 

> The authors missed to refer to recent works that detect fish and may allow to detect aquatic invertebrates too. Please see key works: D. Rout et al., "Underwater Visual Surveillance: A Comprehensive Survey", Ocean Engineering , October 2024. M. Kapoor, et al., “Graph-based Moving Object Segmentation for Underwater Videos using Semi-supervised Learning”, Computer Vision and Image Understanding, 2025. M. Nissar, et al. “NICASU: Neurotransmitter Inspired Cognitive AI Architecture for Surveillance Underwater”, IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence, March 2025.

We appreciate the other suggestions of other contemporary papers on this topic and will integrate them into our paper upon revision.
